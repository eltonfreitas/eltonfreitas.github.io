Elton Eduardo Freitas

Postdoctoral Researcher, Center for Collective Learning, IAST, Toulouse School of Economics
Associate Professor of Economics, Federal University of Sergipe (UFS), Brazil

elton.freitas@academico.ufs.breltonfreitas.github.ioGoogle Scholar 5jq6W3AAAAAJORCID 0000-0001-8887-4471

Economist working on the economics of productive transformation and regional development: how firms, productive capabilities, market structures and production networks shape the economic transformation of cities and regions, and how industrial and place-based policies can foster productivity, diversification and inclusive development. I combine large-scale Brazilian administrative data (RAIS, SECEX, CAGED), applied econometrics and economic complexity, with over 15 years of experience across academia, government and industry.
