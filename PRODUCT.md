# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with a little vanilla JS, hosted on GitHub Pages (eltonfreitas.github.io). No build step. Two language trees: /en/ and /pt/.

## Users

Confirmed by the owner, all three matter:

- Peers and committees: researchers, co-authors, hiring and grant committees checking papers, CV and research line.
- Students: undergraduate and graduate students at UFS and elsewhere looking for class notes, datasets and supervision.
- Press and policymakers: journalists and public managers who need a plain explanation of the work and a way to make contact.

## Product Purpose

The personal academic site of Elton Eduardo Freitas, economist. It lets a visitor learn in seconds who he is, what he studies, read or reach his papers, take a CV, and find teaching material. Success: a visitor leaves with one clear idea of his research and a working link to the paper or CV they came for.

## Positioning

Economic complexity applied to Brazilian regions and industries, built from administrative microdata (RAIS, SECEX, CAGED) and turned into policy tools (DataViva, opportunity maps for Belo Horizonte, export strategy for Minas Gerais, Sergipe textile chain). Few scholars combine CCL/IAST complexity research with this much applied regional and industrial policy work in Brazil.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual EN/PT with separate URLs (/en/, /pt/); English is the default.
- Citation metrics (citations, h-index, i10-index) must stay on the site and update automatically from Google Scholar. Scholar has no official API; automation must keep the last good values when a fetch fails.
- The owner dislikes em dashes in site text.
- Two CVs, English for /en/ and Portuguese for /pt/, generated from cv/en.html and cv/pt.html; no phone number and no third-party referee emails.
- Projects section lists only ongoing projects. Awards section lists every award.
- The owner's phone number is never published. The site's CV (files/CV_Elton_Freitas.pdf) is the English SOAS version with the phone removed.

## Brand Commitments

- The owner asked for his own visual style on the site: the style he uses on social media (skill estilo-redes-elton). Ground #0B0E18, main text #F6F6F2, secondary #C4CAD9, one accent yellow #FFCE3A, card #1C2336; Inter (Display Black for titles and numbers, SemiBold and Regular for text) and Caveat Bold only for short handwritten notes; the hand-drawn stroke (one circle around the main number, an underline on a key phrase, a short yellow note at -2 degrees) used sparingly; photos framed with rounded corners and yellow corner marks, natural colour. ## Product Principles

1. The work leads; chrome stays out of its way.
2. Every paper is reachable in one click.
3. Plain language first, technical terms second, so students and press can follow.
4. Both languages are first-class, written, not machine-swapped.
5. Nothing on the page that needs manual upkeep unless it updates itself.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast and visible keyboard focus. Many visitors read on phones over Brazilian mobile networks, so pages must stay light.
